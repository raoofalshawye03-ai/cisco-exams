An organization is granted a block of addresses with the beginning address 14.24.74.0/24. The organization needs to have 3 subblocks of addresses for its three subnets:
(i) One subblock of 120 addresses.
(ii) One subblock of 50 addresses.
(iii) One subblock of 10 addresses.
Design the subnets and draw the subnetwork diagram.
